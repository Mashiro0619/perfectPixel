# Perfect Pixel — Third-Party & Upstream Notices

## Upstream Algorithm (`perfectPixel`)

- Original algorithm by **theAmusing** and contributors (`https://github.com/theamusing/perfectPixel`, MIT License).
- Fork bugfixes & validation reference: `https://github.com/Mashiro0619/perfectPixel` (`6e2de13`).
- Refactored into a standalone TypeScript Web/Node engine (`@mashirotech/perfect-pixel`) with RGBA support, single-pass Sobel projections, power-of-two `realTransform` FFT acceleration, and per-image prepared caching.
